Week 28 · Summer

Night under the stars

Sleep outside with no tent on Friday night. Wake up with the sun on Saturday.

Total time
Friday night to Saturday morning
Cost
$0 to $150

The morning

On Friday night you all sleep in the backyard with no tent. You lie on your backs and look at the stars until everybody falls asleep. On Saturday the sun wakes you up, and you cook breakfast outside.

Get ready

Pick a clear, dry night. Turn off the sprinklers. Find a flat spot away from the street lights. Lay out the sleeping bags and pads before dark. If you don’t have a yard, book a campsite.

What you need

  • A tarp for the ground
  • A sleeping pad and a sleeping bag for each of you
  • Pillows
  • A second tarp or a blanket to pull over the bags if the dew is heavy
  • Bug spray
  • A flashlight for each of them
  • Warm hats
  • A camp stove or the grill for breakfast
  • A skillet and a spatula
Breakfast for four
Eggs8
Bacon8 slices
Bread4 slices
Butter1 tablespoon
Orange juice

How to do it

  1. Spread the tarp. Put the pads on it and the bags on the pads.
  2. Everybody uses the bathroom and puts on bug spray.
  3. Lie on your backs and turn off the flashlights. Give your eyes 20 minutes to adjust.
  4. They count the stars they can name. You point out the Big Dipper.
  5. Stay out all night. If one of them wants to go in, walk them in and come back.
  6. Wake up with the sun.
  7. Cook the bacon and eggs outside and eat in your sleeping bags.
Keep it safe

Check the forecast, and move inside if you hear thunder or it starts to rain. Keep a flashlight within reach of everybody.

With kids under 6

Set up on the porch or right outside the back door.

With kids 6 and up

They lay out the pads and the bags and find the Big Dipper on their own. Hand them the spatula for the eggs.
