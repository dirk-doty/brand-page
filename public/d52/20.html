Week 20 · Spring

Climb the tree

Pick a big tree with thick limbs. Agree on how high before they start. They climb it and tie a ribbon there.

Total time
About 1 1/2 hours
Cost
$0 to $10

The morning

You find a big tree with low, thick limbs. You agree on a height. They climb up and tie a ribbon there, and you take the photo from the ground.

Get ready

Pick a healthy hardwood such as an oak or a maple. Look for limbs that start low and are thicker than your arm. Stay away from power lines, dead branches and nests. Go on a dry day. If the tree is in a park, check that climbing is allowed.

What you need

  • A bright ribbon for each of them
  • Sneakers with good grip
  • Your phone for the photo

How to do it

  1. Walk around the tree together and look up for dead limbs. If you see any above where they’ll climb, pick another tree.
  2. Agree on how high each of them can go. Point to the limb.
  3. Teach three points of contact. Two hands and a foot, or two feet and a hand, stay on the tree at all times.
  4. They climb close to the trunk, on limbs thicker than your arm.
  5. You stand below and watch every move.
  6. They tie the ribbon and climb down the same way they went up.
  7. Take the photo from the ground with the ribbon in it.
Keep it safe

They can fall, so they only climb on a dry day and never on a dead limb. Nobody goes higher than the limb you agreed on. If they get stuck, talk them down one move at a time.

With kids under 6

Their feet stay within your reach.

With kids 6 and up

They plan the route from the ground. They call out each move before they make it.
