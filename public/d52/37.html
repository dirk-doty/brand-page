Week 37 · Summer

Meteor shower

Wake them at 4:00am in August. Lie on a blanket and count shooting stars.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$0

The morning

You wake your kids at 4:00am during the Perseid meteor shower. Everybody lies on a blanket in the dark and looks up. They count shooting stars until the sky gets light.

Get ready

The Perseids peak around August 12 every year. Pick the Saturday closest to it. Find a dark spot away from town lights, with a wide view of the sky. Check for clouds on Friday night. Set out the blankets and warm clothes before bed.

What you need

  • A big blanket or a tarp
  • A sleeping bag or a blanket for each of you
  • Pillows
  • Warm clothes
  • Bug spray
  • A flashlight with a red light
  • A thermos of hot chocolate and cups (the recipe is on week 2)

How to do it

  1. Get up at 3:45am, make the hot chocolate and fill the thermos. Wake them at 4:00am and carry the little ones to the car in their blankets.
  2. Spread the blanket and lie on your backs, with your feet pointing northeast.
  3. Turn off every light and put the phones away. Give your eyes 20 minutes to adjust.
  4. Look at the whole sky. Don’t stare at one spot.
  5. They call out each shooting star and keep count.
  6. Pour the hot chocolate when the first one of them gets cold.
  7. Stay until the sky starts to get light.
Keep it safe

Park well off the road and stay away from traffic. Bring warm layers, because it gets cold before dawn, even in August.

With kids under 6

Do it in the backyard so they can go back to bed.

With kids 6 and up

They keep their own count and point to where each one started. Whoever counts the most picks breakfast.
