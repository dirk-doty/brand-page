Week 23 · Spring

When you were their age

Show them a photo of you at their age. Do what you did for fun back then, and tell them the stories.

Total time
2 to 4 hours
Cost
$0 to $40

The morning

You show your kids a photo of you at their age. Then you all do what you did for fun back then. It could be playing catch, shooting baskets, skipping rocks or building Legos. You tell them the stories while you do it.

Get ready

Ask your family for photos of you as a kid. Pick something you did for fun every chance you got. Find a place close to home where you can do it. Get the gear together on Friday. Think of three stories to tell.

What you need

  • A photo of you at their age
  • The gear for what you picked
  • Snacks and water
  • Your phone for photos

How to do it

  1. Put the photo on the table at breakfast. They guess how old you were.
  2. Tell them what you did with a free Saturday at that age.
  3. Take them to do it.
  4. Do it with them the way you did it back then. Then let them try it their way.
  5. Tell your three stories. Make one of them about a time it went wrong.
  6. Take a photo of them doing it.
  7. At home, put their photo next to yours.
Keep it safe

Use the safety gear you’d use today, even if you never wore it back then. Put helmets on for bikes and life jackets on near deep water.

With kids under 6

Pick the part of it they can do, and keep it to an hour.

With kids 6 and up

They ask you three questions about being their age. They take a photo of you doing it.
