Week 18 · Spring

Change a tire

They jack up the car and change the tire. You hand them the tools.

Total time
About 1 1/2 hours
Cost
$0 to $60

The morning

In the driveway, your kids take a wheel off the car and put the spare on. Then they put the wheel back. You hand them tools and watch.

Get ready

Find the jack, the lug wrench and the spare in your car. Look up the jacking points in the owner’s manual. Check the air in the spare. If your wheels have a locking nut, find the key. If your car has no spare, take one wheel off and put the same one back on. If it has no jack either, borrow a jack and a lug wrench.

What you need

  • The car’s jack and lug wrench
  • The spare and the owner’s manual
  • A jack stand
  • Two wheel chocks or two bricks
  • Gloves for each of you
  • Something to kneel on
  • A cup for the lug nuts
  • A tire gauge

How to do it

  1. Park on flat pavement and set the parking brake. They chock the wheel at the opposite corner.
  2. With the tire still on the ground, they loosen each lug nut half a turn. You loosen any nut they can’t.
  3. They set the jack at the point the manual shows and raise the car until the tire is an inch or two off the ground.
  4. You set the jack stand next to the jack, at a support point from the manual.
  5. They take the nuts off and drop them in the cup. Then they pull the wheel off.
  6. They lift the spare on and tighten the nuts by hand in a star pattern.
  7. You pull the jack stand. They lower the car and tighten the nuts with the wrench in the same star pattern.
  8. Do it all again to put the regular wheel back on. You check every nut at the end.
Keep it safe

Nobody puts any part of their body under the car while it’s up. That includes hands and feet. A wheel is heavy, so keep their feet clear when it comes off.

With kids under 6

They set the chocks and hold the cup of lug nuts.

With kids 6 and up

They find the jacking points in the manual and do every step. You only step in for a stuck nut, the jack stand and the last check of every nut.
