Week 21 · Spring

Bury a time capsule

Each of you writes a letter. Bury the letters and pick the day you’ll dig them up.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$20 to $60

The morning

Everybody writes a letter to read years from now. You seal the letters in a pipe and bury it in the yard. Then you pick the day you’ll dig it up and put it on the calendar.

Get ready

Call 811 a few business days ahead so the buried lines get marked. Pick a spot you’ll still be able to get to. Buy the pipe parts and have the store cut the pipe to 18 inches. If you rent or might move, plan to keep the capsule on a closet shelf.

What you need

  • 18 inches of 4 inch PVC pipe
  • A glue-on cap
  • A threaded cleanout fitting with a plug
  • PVC cement
  • Paper and pens
  • A photo from this year
  • Zip bags
  • A few silica gel packs
  • A shovel
  • A flat stone for a marker
  • Your phone for the photo

How to do it

  1. Each of you writes a letter. Write down what a normal Saturday is like right now.
  2. Add a photo from this year and one small thing each.
  3. Seal everything in zip bags with the silica packs.
  4. You glue the cap on one end of the pipe and the cleanout fitting on the other. Let the cement set.
  5. They load the bags and screw in the plug.
  6. They dig a hole two feet deep. Bury the capsule and set the stone on top.
  7. Take a photo of the spot and write down where it is.
  8. Pick the day you’ll dig it up. Put it on the calendar.
Keep it safe

Don’t dig until 811 has marked the yard. You handle the PVC cement, and you use it outside.

With kids under 6

They draw a picture and trace their hand.

With kids 6 and up

They write a full page and guess what will be different when you dig it up.
