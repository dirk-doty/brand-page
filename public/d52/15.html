Week 15 · Spring

Plant their tree

Plant a tree for each kid. Take a photo of them next to it every year.

Total time
2 to 3 hours
Cost
$40 to $200 a tree

The morning

Each of them plants a tree in your yard. They dig the hole, you set the tree in it, and they water it. Then you take the first photo of them standing next to it.

Get ready

Call 811 a few business days ahead. It’s free, and they mark the buried lines in your yard. Pick a spot with room for a full grown tree. Buy a tree for each of them at a local nursery and ask what grows well where you live. Wait until the ground has thawed. If you don’t have a yard, plant each tree in a big pot.

What you need

  • A tree for each of them
  • A shovel for each of you
  • A tape measure
  • A utility knife
  • A hose
  • Two or three bags of mulch for each tree
  • A tag with each name on it
  • Your phone for the photo
  • A hand trowel for kids under 6

How to do it

  1. Find the root flare. That’s where the trunk widens at the bottom. Brush the soil off it.
  2. They measure how tall the root ball is.
  3. They dig a hole two to three times as wide as the root ball and no deeper than it is tall.
  4. Take off the pot, the burlap and any wire. Lift the tree by the root ball and set it in the hole.
  5. Check that the root flare sits level with the ground or a little above it.
  6. They fill the hole with the same dirt, press it down and water it.
  7. Spread mulch two to four inches deep in a three foot circle. Keep it off the trunk.
  8. Hang the name tags. Take a photo of each of them next to their tree.
  9. Water every week for the first year. Take the same photo every year.
Keep it safe

Don’t dig until 811 has marked the yard. You do the heavy lifting.

With kids under 6

They scoop dirt with a hand trowel, water the tree and spread the mulch.

With kids 6 and up

They dig the whole hole and check the root flare with you. The weekly watering is their job for the first year.
