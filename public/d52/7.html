Week 07 · Winter

Breakfast in an igloo

Build a snow shelter big enough for everybody. Eat breakfast inside it.

Total time
2 to 3 hours on Friday and 2 to 3 hours on Saturday
Cost
$0 to $40

The morning

You pile a big mound of snow on Friday and let it set overnight. On Saturday you hollow it out and everybody eats breakfast inside.

Get ready

You need a second adult for this one, so invite another dad and his kids, or bring Mom. You need a foot or two of snow on the ground. Cut 25 sticks, each 12 inches long. Pile the mound Friday afternoon. Make the burritos Saturday morning and wrap them in foil. Double the recipe for two families. Make the hot chocolate and fill the thermos.

What you need

  • Two or three shovels
  • A small scoop for the inside
  • Foil for the burritos
  • A thermos of hot chocolate and cups (the recipe is on week 2)
  • The 25 sticks
  • A ski pole for the air hole
  • A tarp for the floor
  • Something to sit on
  • Dry gloves for everybody
  • A headlamp
Breakfast burritos for four
Large tortillas4
Eggs, scrambled8
Shredded cheese1 cup
Cooked sausage patties, chopped4
Oranges

How to do it

  1. Friday, mark a circle seven to ten feet across. Pile snow into a mound about five feet tall and pack it as you go.
  2. Let it set overnight.
  3. Saturday, push the sticks straight into the mound all over, until each one is buried.
  4. Dig a low door on the side away from the wind.
  5. One adult hollows it out from the inside. All the kids haul the snow away from the door. Stop digging wherever you hit the end of a stick. That keeps the walls a foot thick.
  6. Poke an air hole through the top with the ski pole, about as wide as your fist.
  7. Lay down the tarp, crawl in and eat.
Keep it safe

The roof can collapse, so one adult stays outside any time somebody is digging inside. Nobody climbs on the roof. Keep the air hole and the door open, and never light a stove or a candle in there. Never let them dig into the piles a snowplow leaves by the road.

With kids under 6

They pile snow and pack it down. They go inside after you’ve checked it.

With kids 6 and up

They mark the circle, push in the sticks and haul the snow. They lay the tarp and hand out the burritos.
