Week 38 · Summer

Build a go-kart

Build a go-kart or a soapbox racer with a brake. Put helmets on and race it down a quiet hill with no cars.

Total time
4 to 5 hours
Cost
$100 to $300

The morning

You and your kids build a wooden go-kart with four wheels, rope steering and a brake. They sand it, paint a number on it and name it. Then they race it down a quiet hill, one driver at a time.

Get ready

Print a soapbox go-kart plan that has a brake, or buy a kit. Buy the lumber, four wheels and the hardware on the plan. Find a gentle, paved hill with no cars and a long flat stretch at the bottom. An empty park path works.

What you need

  • The printed plan or the kit
  • The lumber, wheels, axles and bolts on the plan
  • A cordless drill
  • A saw, for you only
  • Wrenches
  • Rope for the steering
  • Sandpaper
  • Paint and a brush
  • Your phone to time the runs
  • A notebook and a pencil
  • Safety glasses for everybody
  • A helmet for each driver
  • Closed toe shoes
  • Gloves

How to do it

  1. They measure and mark the boards from the plan. You make the cuts.
  2. Bolt the frame, the seat and the axles together the way the plan shows.
  3. Mount the wheels. Tie on the steering rope.
  4. Build the brake from the plan and bolt it on.
  5. They sand it, paint a number on it and give it a name.
  6. You ride it down the hill first and test the brake.
  7. They race it one at a time. You stand at the bottom.
  8. Time every run and write the times down.
Keep it safe

Glasses stay on while the saw or the drill is running. Every driver wears a helmet and closed toe shoes. Nobody rides it without a working brake. Use a hill with no cars, no cross streets and a flat place to stop. One driver rides at a time, sitting down.

With kids under 6

Pull them on flat ground with a rope.

With kids 6 and up

They read the plan, measure and mark every board and tighten the bolts. One of them runs the timer at the bottom.
