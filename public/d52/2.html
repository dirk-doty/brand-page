Week 02 · Winter

Sunrise summit

Hike up in the dark with headlamps. Drink hot chocolate at the top while the sun comes up.

Total time
3 to 4 hours
Cost
$0 to $40

The morning

You wake your kids while it’s still dark and hike a short trail by headlamp. You reach the top before sunrise. Then everybody drinks hot chocolate and watches the sun come up.

Get ready

Pick a trail you’ve hiked in daylight, one to two miles to the top. Look up the sunrise time and the forecast. Charge the headlamps and lay out everybody’s clothes the night before. Tell someone where you’re going.

What you need

  • A headlamp for each of you and spare batteries
  • A big thermos and cups
  • A warm jacket, hat and gloves for everybody
  • Something dry to sit on
  • A small first aid kit and your phone
  • A carrier for kids under 6
Hot chocolate for four
Whole milk4 cups
Cocoa powder1/3 cup
Sugar1/3 cup
SaltA pinch
Vanilla1 teaspoon

How to do it

  1. Count back from sunrise. Give yourself one hour for each mile, plus 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the milk and whisk in the cocoa, sugar, salt and vanilla. Fill the thermos.
  3. At the trailhead, dress them in the car so they stay warm.
  4. Put your oldest in front and walk at the back, where you can see everybody. Stop every 15 minutes.
  5. Get to the top 15 minutes early. That’s when the sky turns colors.
  6. Put jackets on as soon as you stop. Then pour the hot chocolate.
  7. Take your photo when the sun clears the horizon. Hike down in daylight.
Keep it safe

In the dark, only hike a trail you already know. It’s colder and windier on top, so pack an extra layer for everybody. If one of them starts shivering hard or stumbling, head down.

With kids under 6

Keep it under a mile and bring a carrier.

With kids 6 and up

They lead the way up. Have them work out what time you leave.
