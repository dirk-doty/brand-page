Week 06 · Winter

Build a sled run

Pack a sled track with banked turns. Time every run and race it until lunch.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$0 to $100

The morning

You and your kids turn a hill into a sled track. You pack the track, build a banked turn and time every run until lunch.

Get ready

Wait for six inches of snow or more. Pick a hill with a long, flat bottom and no trees, fences, roads or water at the end of it. Find a helmet for each of you. Ski helmets and bike helmets both work.

What you need

  • Two or three snow shovels
  • Sleds you can steer
  • A helmet for each of you
  • Sticks to mark the track
  • Your phone to time the runs

How to do it

  1. Walk the hill and pick where the track goes. Mark both sides with sticks. Clear every rock and branch out of it.
  2. Pack a track two to three feet wide. Step down it sideways to press the snow flat.
  3. Build the banked turn. Pile snow on the outside of the curve about two feet high and pack it hard.
  4. You take the first few runs slowly to pack the track and check it.
  5. Say the rules: sit up, feet first, one sled at a time. Walk back up along the side.
  6. Time every run. Write the record in the snow.
Keep it safe

Put a helmet on everybody. Ride sitting up and feet first. Nobody rides head first. Keep jumps out of the track. Keep the bottom of the hill clear of people.

With kids under 6

They ride in your lap, on the gentle part of the hill.

With kids 6 and up

They plan the track and shovel the banked turn. One of them runs the timer on your phone.
