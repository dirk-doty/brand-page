Week 05 · Winter

New Year’s: the polar plunge

Walk into cold water together on the first Saturday of the year. You go first. Turn around and walk right back out to dry towels and hot chocolate.

Total time
2 to 3 hours
Cost
$0 to $40

The morning

You start the year in cold water. Everybody walks in holding hands, turns around and walks out. Then it’s towels, dry clothes and a hot breakfast.

Get ready

Look for an organized plunge near you. Many of those have medics and a dive team on site. If you go on your own, pick a beach you know with a gentle, firm bottom. Lay out dry clothes in the order they go on. Make the hot chocolate and fill the thermos. If you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, ask your doctor first.

What you need

  • Water shoes for everybody
  • Two towels each
  • A big coat or a robe each
  • Hats
  • Dry clothes
  • Life jackets for them in a lake or the ocean
  • A thermos of hot chocolate and cups
  • A warm car parked close
Hot chocolate for four
Whole milk4 cups
Cocoa powder1/3 cup
Sugar1/3 cup
SaltA pinch
Vanilla1 teaspoon

How to do it

  1. Warm up the car. Set the towels and coats at the water’s edge.
  2. Say the plan out loud: walk in to your waist, turn around, walk out. The younger ones go knee deep.
  3. You go first. Hold hands.
  4. The cold will make you gasp. Breathe out slow and keep every head above water.
  5. Stay in for a few seconds. Then walk out.
  6. Wet clothes come off right away. Dry layers and hats go on.
  7. Drink the hot chocolate in the car. Then go get a hot breakfast.
Keep it safe

Nobody jumps or dives in. The first minute is the dangerous part, because cold water makes you gasp and breathe fast. Never go in through a hole in the ice. They cool down faster than you do, so get them dry first.

With kids under 6

They only get their feet wet. Hold their hand at the edge.

With kids 6 and up

They hold your hand and follow you in. They count to five out loud before you all turn around.
