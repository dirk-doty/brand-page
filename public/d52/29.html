Week 29 · Summer

Rope swing

Hang a rope swing from a thick, live branch over soft ground. You test it first.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$40 to $100

The morning

You and your kids pick the tree and the branch. You hang the rope and tie on a seat. You swing on it first, and then they take turns until lunch.

Get ready

Find a healthy hardwood tree, such as an oak or a maple. Pick a live branch at least eight inches thick, with soft ground under it and nothing to hit. Buy the rope and a tree swing strap. Ask a second adult to come hold the ladder.

What you need

  • A tree swing strap kit made to hold an adult
  • A braided polyester rope, at least half an inch thick and a few feet longer than the branch is high
  • A round wooden disc seat with a hole in the middle
  • A ladder
  • A second adult to hold the ladder
  • Gloves

How to do it

  1. Look the branch over together. It should have leaves, with no cracks, no dead wood and no fungus.
  2. Pick a spot on the branch at least three feet out from the trunk.
  3. You climb the ladder and wrap the strap around the branch. The other adult holds the ladder.
  4. Clip or tie the rope to the strap.
  5. They thread the rope through the seat. You tie a big knot under it, about two feet off the ground.
  6. Hang on the rope with your full weight. Then swing on it yourself.
  7. They take turns, one at a time.
Keep it safe

Only you go up the ladder. Never hang a swing over water you haven’t checked, over pavement or over rocks. Look at the branch, the strap and the rope before every use, and take the swing down if any of them looks worn.

With kids under 6

Tie the seat lower and push them gently.

With kids 6 and up

They check the branch with you and thread the rope through the seat. They tell you how high to tie the knot.
