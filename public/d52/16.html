Week 16 · Spring

The rain hike

Wait for a downpour and go hiking anyway. Stomp in every puddle.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$0 to $100

The morning

You wait for a rainy Saturday and go hiking in it. They stomp in every puddle and race leaf boats down the trail. Then everybody changes into dry clothes at the car and drinks hot chocolate.

Get ready

Watch the forecast for steady rain with no thunder. Check for flood warnings. Pick a loop of one to three miles with no creek crossings. Put dry clothes and a towel for everybody in the car. Make the hot chocolate and fill the thermos.

What you need

  • A rain jacket and rain pants for each of you
  • Rubber boots
  • Wool socks
  • A full change of clothes and a towel each
  • A ruler
  • A thermos of hot chocolate and cups (the recipe is on week 2)

How to do it

  1. Check the radar before you leave. If it shows lightning, wait.
  2. Put the rain gear on at the car.
  3. Hike the loop. They stomp in every puddle.
  4. They find the biggest puddle and measure how deep it is.
  5. Race leaf boats in the water running down the trail.
  6. Back at the car, everybody changes into dry clothes.
  7. Pour the hot chocolate.
Keep it safe

If you hear thunder, get back to the car. Stay out of creeks, because they rise fast in the rain. If one of them starts shivering, head back.

With kids under 6

Walk half a mile and let them stay at the best puddle.

With kids 6 and up

They pick the loop and check the radar with you. Then they lead the hike.
