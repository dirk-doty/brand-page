Week 09 · Winter

Build their workbench

Build a workbench at their height. Hang their own hammer on it.

Total time
3 to 4 hours
Cost
$60 to $120

The morning

You build a real workbench out of 2x4s, sized for them. They mark the cuts, drive the screws and sand it. When it’s done, their own hammer hangs on the side.

Get ready

Have them stand up straight and measure from the floor to their wrist. That’s the height of the top. If they’re different heights, measure the tallest and find a step stool for the others. Have the store cut the plywood. Charge the drill.

What you need

  • A cordless drill with a 1/8 inch bit and a driver bit
  • A saw, for you only
  • A tape measure, a pencil and a marker
  • A speed square
  • Two clamps
  • Safety glasses and ear protection for everybody
  • Four 8 foot 2x4s
  • A 3/4 inch plywood top, 36 by 20 inches
  • A plywood shelf, 29 1/2 by 20 inches
  • A pound of 2 1/2 inch screws
  • Two hooks
  • Sandpaper
  • A 16 ounce hammer

How to do it

  1. Cut four pieces at 36 inches and four at 17 inches. Cut four legs, each 3/4 inch shorter than the height you measured. They mark each line with the square, and you cut.
  2. Build two frames. Each one is two 36 inch pieces with a 17 inch piece between them at each end. Drill a pilot hole for every screw and put two screws in each corner.
  3. Lay one frame flat on the floor. Stand a leg inside each corner, with its wide side against the 17 inch piece.
  4. Screw through the frame into each leg, two screws from the front and two from the end.
  5. Mark each leg six inches down from the end that’s sticking up. Slide the second frame over the legs to the marks. Clamp it and screw it to each leg the same way.
  6. Flip the bench over. Check that it doesn’t rock.
  7. Screw the top down every eight inches. Set the shelf on the lower frame, between the legs.
  8. They sand every edge. Screw in the hooks and hang the hammer.
  9. Everybody signs the underside and writes the date.
Keep it safe

Glasses and ear protection stay on. You make every saw cut. They drive screws with two hands on the drill, at low speed.

With kids under 6

They hold the square, hand you screws and sand.

With kids 6 and up

They read the tape, mark every cut and drive every screw. They check each corner with the square.
