Week 22 · Spring

Record the old stories

Take them to breakfast with the oldest person in your family. Ask about the old days and record it on your phone.

Total time
2 to 3 hours
Cost
$20 to $60

The morning

You take your kids to breakfast with the oldest person in your family. They ask the questions and your phone records the answers. Afterward you send the recording to the whole family.

Get ready

Call and set the date. Ask if it’s OK to record. Write ten questions with your kids. Charge your phone and clear some space on it. Find a few old family photos.

What you need

  • Your phone with a voice recorder app
  • The list of questions
  • Old family photos
  • Money for breakfast

How to do it

  1. Pick a quiet table. Set the phone in the middle and start recording.
  2. Start with the easy ones: a full name, a birthplace and a year.
  3. They ask the questions. What was your house like? What did you do on Saturdays? What was your first job? What did you get in trouble for?
  4. Put the old photos on the table and ask who’s in them.
  5. When a story gets going, let it run and skip the rest of the list.
  6. Record for 30 minutes to an hour.
  7. Back the recording up that day and send it to the family.
Keep it safe

Ask before you record. If a question is hard to answer, move on to the next one. Stop before anybody gets tired.

With kids under 6

They ask one question each and bring a drawing to give away.

With kids 6 and up

They write their own questions and run the recorder. Teach them to ask, “What happened next?”
