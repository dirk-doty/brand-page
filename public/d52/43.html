Week 43 · Fall

Parking lot bike race

They chalk a race course in an empty parking lot and race it on bikes. You race too.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$0 to $10

The morning

Your kids draw a race course with chalk in an empty parking lot. They race it on bikes, one rider at a time, while you run the stopwatch. Then you race it and they time you.

Get ready

Find a big lot that sits empty on Saturday mornings, at a school or an office. Ask the owner if you can use it. Buy sidewalk chalk. Pump the tires and check the brakes on every bike.

What you need

  • A bike and a helmet for each of you
  • Sidewalk chalk
  • Four cones or empty boxes
  • A stopwatch or your phone
  • A pump
  • Water

How to do it

  1. Walk the lot together. Check for poles, curbs, potholes and loose gravel. Pick the smoothest stretch, away from the poles.
  2. They draw the course with chalk: a start line, wide turns, a row of cones to weave through and a finish line.
  3. Fit the helmets. Each one sits level, two fingers above the eyebrows, with the strap snug.
  4. Everybody rides one slow lap to learn the course, one rider at a time.
  5. They race one rider at a time. You run the stopwatch and write each time on the pavement.
  6. You race it. They run the stopwatch.
  7. Everybody gets three tries and races to beat their own best time.
  8. They draw a new course and you race again.
Keep it safe

Only one rider is on the course at a time. Helmets stay on, buckled. Everybody who is waiting stands off to the side. Stop if a car comes into the lot.

With kids under 6

Draw them a short loop of their own, away from the race course. They ride it on a balance bike, a tricycle or a scooter.

With kids 6 and up

They design the course and decide the rules. They run the stopwatch for your laps.
