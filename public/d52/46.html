The $20 morning

Hand them $20. They plan the whole morning and you go along with it.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$20

The morning

You hand your kids a $20 bill. They decide where you go, what you do and what you eat. You drive and go along with the whole plan.

Get ready

Tell them on Friday so they have a night to plan. Give them the rules: stay within 30 minutes of home, stay inside $20, and everybody has to be able to do it. Get a $20 bill.

What you need

  • A $20 bill
  • A full tank of gas
  • Your phone for photos

How to do it

  1. Hand over the $20 at breakfast.
  2. They tell you the plan. Ask questions, and don’t change it.
  3. You drive. They give the directions.
  4. They pay for everything and count the change.
  5. If the money runs out, the rest of the morning has to be free.
  6. Take a photo at every stop.
  7. On the way home, they tell you what they’d do with $20 next time.
Keep it safe

You can say no to anything that isn’t safe. Everything else, you go along with.

With kids under 6

Give them two choices at each stop and let them pick.

With kids 6 and up

They hold the money and add up what’s left after every stop.
