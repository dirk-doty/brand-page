Week 51 · Fall

Dads against kids

Get a few dads together and play all the kids in a full game of soccer or kickball. The winners keep the trophy for a year.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$10 to $30

The morning

You call a few dads and pick a field. All the dads play all the kids in a full game of soccer or kickball. The winners take the trophy home and keep it for a year.

Get ready

Text three or four dads this week and set the time. Pick a park with an open field. Buy or make a trophy. Decide on soccer or kickball.

What you need

  • A ball
  • Cones or shirts for goals and bases
  • A trophy
  • A marker
  • A whistle
  • Water for everybody
  • Orange slices for halftime

How to do it

  1. Set up the field. Make it small enough for the youngest player.
  2. Even things out. The dads play with one fewer player, or they only score with their weak foot.
  3. Play two halves of 20 minutes, or seven innings.
  4. Every kid plays the whole game.
  5. Stop at halftime for water and oranges.
  6. Shake hands at the end.
  7. Hand over the trophy. Write the year and the winner on the bottom.
Keep it safe

The dads play at half speed around the little ones. No slide tackles and no hard throws at runners. Everybody drinks water at halftime.

With kids under 6

They get a free kick or a free base every time they touch the ball.

With kids 6 and up

They pick a captain and choose their own positions. If they win, the captain carries the trophy home.
