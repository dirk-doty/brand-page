Week 03 · Winter

The Christmas record

Buy a cheap or old record player and let them pick a Christmas album. Play it for the family all through the holidays.

Total time
2 to 3 hours
Cost
$5 to $120

The morning

You and your kids shop for a cheap or old record player and one Christmas album. They pick the album. At home they set the record on the player and lower the needle, and the family listens to it together.

Get ready

Ask your family and your neighbors if anybody has an old record player in a closet. If nobody does, look at thrift stores and online listings for one with speakers built in. Find a record store or a thrift store with a bin of Christmas albums. Clear a flat shelf at home near an outlet.

What you need

  • A record player with speakers built in
  • A Christmas album
  • A soft cloth to wipe the record
  • Money for the player and the album

How to do it

  1. At the store, look the cord over for cracks. Then plug the player in before you pay. Check that the platter spins and that the arm has a needle.
  2. They flip through the Christmas albums and agree on one. Slide the record out and check it for deep scratches.
  3. At home, set the player on the flat shelf. You plug it in.
  4. Show them how to hold a record by the edges and the label.
  5. Switch the speed to 33. They set the record on the platter, start it and lower the needle onto the outside edge.
  6. Listen to side one together. They flip it for side two.
  7. Slide the record back into its sleeve and stand it on its edge. Play it for the family all through the holidays.
Keep it safe

Check the cord on an old player for cracks before you plug it in. Set the player where the youngest can’t pull it down.

With kids under 6

They pick the album by its cover. They start the record with your hand on theirs.

With kids 6 and up

They handle the record and lower the needle themselves. They’re in charge of flipping it.
