Week 50 · Fall

Their first business

They make something and sell it at the end of the driveway. They keep the money.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$10 to $30

The morning

Your kids make something to sell. It could be hot chocolate, cookies, firewood bundles or painted rocks. They set up a table at the end of the driveway and run it. They keep every dollar they make.

Get ready

On Friday they pick what to sell and make a sign. You buy the supplies. Get $10 in ones and quarters for change. Tell the neighbors what time they open.

What you need

  • A folding table and chairs
  • A big sign
  • What they’re selling
  • A cash box with change
  • A notebook and a pencil
  • A thermos or a cooler if they’re selling drinks

How to do it

  1. They add up what the supplies cost.
  2. They set a price that makes money on each sale.
  3. They set up the table and hang the sign where cars can see it.
  4. They greet every customer, take the money and make change.
  5. They write down every sale.
  6. At closing time, they count the cash box.
  7. They pay you back for the supplies. What’s left is theirs.
Keep it safe

Stay where you can see the table. The table sits on your lawn or driveway, back from the street. They wash their hands before they handle food.

With kids under 6

They hand each customer the order and say thank you.

With kids 6 and up

They work out the cost, set the price and run the cash box alone. They decide what to do with the profit.
