Week 41 · Fall

Forage and eat it

Pick a bucket of blackberries or apples. Only pick what you know for sure. Make pancakes with them when you get home.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$10 to $40

The morning

You and your kids pick a bucket of blackberries or apples. Back home, they stir the fruit into pancake batter. Everybody eats what they picked.

Get ready

Find a pick-your-own farm or an orchard near you. That’s the easy way to do it. If you pick wild, go with somebody who knows the plants, and get permission for the land. Stay away from roadsides and sprayed fields.

What you need

  • A bucket for each of you
  • Long sleeves and long pants for blackberry thorns
  • Water
  • A colander
  • A knife and a cutting board
  • A mixing bowl and a whisk
  • A skillet or a griddle
  • A spatula
Pancakes for four
Flour1 1/2 cups
Baking powder1 tablespoon
Sugar1 tablespoon
Salt1/2 teaspoon
Milk1 1/4 cups
Egg1
Melted butter3 tablespoons
Berries or chopped apples1 cup
Maple syrup

How to do it

  1. Show them what a ripe one looks like. A ripe blackberry is black all over and comes off with a light pull.
  2. They pick. You check the buckets as they fill.
  3. At home, wash the fruit in the colander. You chop the apples.
  4. They whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in the bowl. Then they stir in the milk, the egg and the butter.
  5. They fold in the fruit.
  6. Cook the pancakes on medium heat, about two minutes a side.
  7. Eat them with syrup.
Keep it safe

Only pick what you know for sure. Some wild berries will make you sick, so anything you can’t name stays on the plant. Nobody eats from the bucket until you’ve checked it. Wash everything first.

With kids under 6

They pick the low branches and stir the batter.

With kids 6 and up

They measure and mix the batter on their own. They flip the pancakes with you standing next to the stove.
