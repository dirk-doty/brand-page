Week 04 · Winter

First pocketknife

Give them their first knife and teach them the rules. Then they whittle a marshmallow stick with it.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$25 to $60

The morning

You hand over their first pocketknife and teach four rules. They practice opening it, closing it and passing it. Then they carve a point on a stick and roast a marshmallow on it.

Get ready

Buy a small knife with a rounded tip and sharpen it. Cut two green sticks for each of them, about three feet long and as thick as your finger. Stay away from anything with poison ivy on it.

What you need

  • The knife
  • A sharpener
  • Two green sticks for each of them
  • A cut glove for the hand that holds the stick
  • A chair for each of you
  • A first aid kit
  • A bag of marshmallows
  • A fire or a grill
  • A vegetable peeler for kids under 6

How to do it

  1. Tell them a knife is a tool, and it only comes out when there’s a job to do.
  2. Teach the four rules. Stop: nobody is within arm’s reach. Away: cut away from your body. Sharp: a sharp, clean knife is the safe one. Store: close it when you’re done.
  3. Show them the safety circle. They hold the closed knife and turn all the way around with their arm out. If anybody steps inside that circle, the knife closes.
  4. They practice opening it, closing it with fingers clear, and passing it closed.
  5. They sit down with elbows on knees. They peel the top ten inches of bark, pushing the blade away from their body.
  6. They shave a point with short strokes.
  7. They close the knife. Then they roast the marshmallow.
Keep it safe

Watch the direction of every stroke. The blade always moves away from their body. The knife closes before anybody stands up. It never goes to school.

With kids under 6

They peel bark with a vegetable peeler while you carve.

With kids 6 and up

They carve a second point with no help from you. Then they teach the four rules back to you.
