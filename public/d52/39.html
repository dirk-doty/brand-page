Week 39 · Summer

Two dads, one fire

Call another dad. Bring all the kids and cook breakfast over one fire.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$30 to $60

The morning

You call another dad and pick a Saturday. Both families meet at a fire pit, and the two of you cook breakfast over one fire. All the kids eat together.

Get ready

Call him this week and set the date. Split the food between you. Pick a fire pit at a park or in one of your backyards. Check for burn bans. Bring dry firewood.

What you need

  • Firewood and kindling
  • Matches
  • A grill grate that sits over the fire
  • A big cast iron skillet
  • A long spatula and tongs
  • Oven mitts
  • An empty can for the grease
  • Plates and forks
  • A bucket of water and a shovel
  • Camp chairs
Breakfast for eight
Bacon2 pounds
Eggs16
Flour tortillas8
Shredded cheese2 cups
Jar of salsa1
Orange juice
Coffee for the dads

How to do it

  1. Build the fire and let it burn down to hot coals. That takes about 30 minutes.
  2. Set the grate over the coals and heat the skillet.
  3. Cook the bacon first. Move it to a plate.
  4. Pour off most of the grease into a can. Scramble the eggs in what’s left.
  5. The older kids warm the tortillas on the grate with the tongs.
  6. Everybody builds a breakfast taco.
  7. Put the fire out. Pour water on it, stir it with the shovel and pour again. Don’t leave until the ashes are cool.
Keep it safe

Keep the little ones three feet back from the fire. One dad stays with the fire the whole time. The water bucket sits next to it.

With kids under 6

They carry the plates and sprinkle the cheese.

With kids 6 and up

They build the fire with the dads and scramble the eggs. They wear the oven mitts at the grate.
