Week 49 · Fall

Teach your thing

Pick the one thing you do best. Spend the morning teaching it to your kids.

Total time
About 3 hours
Cost
$0 to $50

The morning

You pick the one thing you’re best at. It could be casting a fly rod, changing oil, smoking a brisket or playing guitar. You spend the morning teaching it to your kids, one step at a time.

Get ready

Pick the thing. Break it into three or four steps they can do today. Get a second set of gear in their size if you can. Set everything out on Friday night.

What you need

  • Your gear
  • A set of gear they can use
  • A notebook and a pencil
  • Your phone for photos

How to do it

  1. Tell them how you learned it and who taught you.
  2. Show them the whole thing once, at full speed.
  3. Show them the first step slowly. Then they do it.
  4. Fix one thing at a time. Tell them what they did right first.
  5. Add the next step when the first one works.
  6. Before you stop, they do the whole thing once on their own.
  7. They write down what they learned and the date.
Keep it safe

Go over the safety rules for your gear before anybody picks it up. You handle anything sharp, hot or heavy.

With kids under 6

Give them one small job and let them do it the whole morning.

With kids 6 and up

They do the whole thing with no help by the end. Next Saturday, they teach it to somebody else.
