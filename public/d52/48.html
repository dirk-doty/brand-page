Week 48 · Fall

Halloween: power tool pumpkins

Carve pumpkins with a drill and a jigsaw. They draw the faces and scoop the seeds. You run the tools.

Total time
About 2 hours
Cost
$20 to $40

The morning

Each of them picks a pumpkin, scoops it out and draws a face on it. You drill the eyes, freckles and stars and cut the big shapes with a jigsaw. They save the seeds for roasting.

Get ready

Buy a pumpkin for each of them and one for you. Charge the drill. Cover a table in the garage or the yard with newspaper, because this gets messy.

What you need

  • A cordless drill and a few bits, from 1/4 inch to 1 inch
  • A jigsaw with a coarse wood blade
  • Safety glasses for everybody
  • Dry erase markers
  • A big metal spoon
  • A bowl for the seeds
  • Tea lights
  • A lighter
  • Newspaper for the table
  • An old towel for each pumpkin
  • Paper and a pencil for kids 6 and up
Roasted seeds
Pumpkin seeds2 cups
Oil1 tablespoon
Salt1/2 teaspoon

How to do it

  1. Put safety glasses on everybody. They stand an arm’s length back while a tool is running.
  2. Cut the lids with the jigsaw. Angle the blade toward the middle so the lid can’t fall in.
  3. They scoop out the seeds and scrape the walls thin.
  4. They draw the faces with a dry erase marker. Mistakes wipe right off.
  5. Drill the round parts first: eyes, freckles and stars. Then cut the big shapes with the jigsaw.
  6. They push the pieces out and wipe off the marker.
  7. Rinse the seeds and toss them with the oil and salt. Roast them at 300 degrees for about 40 minutes.
  8. Light the tea lights when it gets dark.
Keep it safe

You’re the only one who runs the drill and the jigsaw. Set each pumpkin on a towel so it can’t roll. Pull the drill battery and unplug the jigsaw before your kids go near a pumpkin.

With kids under 6

They scoop, draw and pick where the holes go.

With kids 6 and up

They draw the face on paper first and mark every hole on the pumpkin. They roast the seeds with you.
