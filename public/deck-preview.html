The setup

It’s 6:15pm and you’ve had a long day. Dinner is finally on the table, piping hot lasagna. Your son has been wound up since school, running laps around the kitchen, and you’ve told him twice to stop. Then he clips the corner of the table. The pan slides off, lands face down, and sauce splatters all over the cabinets.

The moment

He didn’t mean to. He looks up at you with wide eyes. You yell: “Are you KIDDING me!? I told you twice!” The whole table freezes. He goes quiet and looks at his feet.

Your response

Now you’re standing over a mess, feeling like the worst dad on the block. You have a minute to decide what kind of night this is, and your son will remember how you respond.

01 · What it is

What is Dad Shift

Dad Shift is thirteen audio cards that live on your phone. Each card helps you drill a common situation.

One card is for the minutes after you yell. Every card walks you through the same four moves: Steady first, Get on their side, Hold the line and Circle back. Press play and the audio talks you through it.

When it counts, you fall to the level of your training.

You turn off the tablet and he melts down.
The boys going at it in the back seat.
You walk in after a rough day and snap at the first noise.
02 · The four moves
01Before
Steady first
Relax your jaw. Take a deep breath and smile.
02During
Get on their side
Kneel down. “You’re frustrated the show’s over?”
03During
Hold the line
Stay down there with him and keep your voice steady. “I know, bud. The tablet’s done for today.”
04After
Circle back
If you lost it before you caught yourself, go back once things are calm. “Hey bud, I yelled and that was wrong. I’m sorry.” Then wrap your arms around him for a big squeeze.
